# Out of scope and known limits (inventory)

One table that gathers the out-of-scope table and each group's "Unmeasured paths and notes" in [scope.md](scope.md), the "Known limitations" in [notes.md](notes.md),
[nonroot.md](../nonroot.md), and what experiments found, sorted by **kind** (an inventory started after v0.8.0 — bpf-mlir v1.0.0 — and kept up to date since).
A test checks the group ids (`G\d+`) and out-of-scope ids (`F\d+`) against the scope tables (every group with unmeasured paths has a row here — the page does not go stale).
"3 kernels" means 7.1.8, 6.12.111 and 6.6.157.

Kinds:

- **(A)** fixable in the go-bpf-mlir frontend or its documents — diagnostic text, chained errors, shape checks, stale documents
- **(B)** depends on bpf-mlir's capabilities and contract — candidate requests (with the amount of demand)
- **(C)** a kernel constraint or kernel behavior — cannot be fixed (documented for users)
- **(D)** just not measured — adding tests (differential tests, fixtures) would fill it
- **(E)** no demand, or left out on purpose — revisited when demand appears
